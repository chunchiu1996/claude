Tips

  • Send from a real company address, and set up SPF/DKIM in your email tool, so the mail isn't marked as spam.
  • Only email people who have agreed to receive it, or who are business contacts you have a reason to reach. Honour “退订” (unsubscribe) replies promptly.
  • Use a new campaign name for each send so you can compare results.
