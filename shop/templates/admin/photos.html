Bulk photo upload

Name each photo after the product's SKU and drop them all here: they're matched to products automatically, resized for the web, and shown in the shop.

  • Upper/lower case doesn't matter. JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and iPhone HEIC photos all work.
  • Uploading a photo with the same name again replaces the old one.
  • Big photos are shrunk automatically, so phone photos are fine.
  • Factories can also paste photos straight into the Excel template: they're picked up on Import.
  • A .zip can hold up to 1,000 photos and {{ max_mb }} MB. Selecting files or a folder has no limit.
