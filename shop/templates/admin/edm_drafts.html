How review works

  1. The AI writes the copy from your catalog and lists anything you should double-check.
  2. Automatic checks flag prices that don't match the catalog, hidden or sold-out products, placeholders and spammy wording.
  3. You edit it, or tell the AI what to change. Send yourself a test.
  4. Approve it with your name. Only then can you copy the HTML into your email tool. Editing it later sends it back for review.

To have drafts written on a schedule, run flask --app wsgi edm-draft --audience buyer --brief "…" from cron. Each one lands here, and you get an email asking you to review it.
