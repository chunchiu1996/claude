Setting it up

Use any email provider that gives you SMTP settings. Good free options:

ProviderSMTP_HOSTPort
Brevo (300/day free)smtp-relay.brevo.com587
Resend (100/day free)smtp.resend.com465
Google Workspace / Gmailsmtp.gmail.com587
Microsoft 365 / Outlooksmtp.office365.com587
  • SMTP_FROM must be an address your provider has verified, e.g. sales@liquidatorusa.com.
  • Gmail needs an app password (Google account → Security → App passwords), not your normal password.
  • So emails don't land in spam, add the SPF and DKIM records your provider shows you in Cloudflare → DNS.
  • Invoices print the PAYMENT_INSTRUCTIONS setting (Zelle, bank transfer, check…).
